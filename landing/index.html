Metric Handbook v1.0
01 / POWER

Critical Power (CP)

The highest power output maintainable for a quasi-steady state without exhaustion. Calculated using a 2-parameter linear model.

02 / CAPACITY

W' Balance

The anaerobic matchbox. A dynamic model of work capacity performed above Critical Power, integrating both discharge and recovery rates.

03 / STRESS

xPower

Accounts for metabolic cost of variable efforts using 4th-power weighting to emphasize the exponential cost of high intensity.

04 / RECOVERY

HRV Status

Measures autonomic balance. A drop in R-R interval variation relative to baseline signals systemic fatigue.

05 / ENGINE

Efficiency Factor

The ratio of normalized output to physiological input. EF = xPower ÷ Avg HR. Quantifies aerobic efficiency gains.

06 / PROGRESS

Fitness (LTS)

An exponentially weighted moving average of daily training stress, typically over a 42-day time constant.
